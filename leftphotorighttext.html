Porsche

Porsche was founded in 1931 by Ferdinand Porsche

Last updated 3 mins ago

1931: Ferdinand Porsche establishes the design firm in Stuttgart. Early projects include developing the precursor to the Volkswagen Beetle for the German masses.

1948: Ferry Porsche, Ferdinand's son, builds the Porsche 356, the first official car bearing the Porsche name, using a lightweight chassis and leftover Volkswagen parts in Gmünd, Austria 1953: The lightweight 550 Spyder arrives as Porsche's first dedicated racing car, scoring major class wins at events like Le Mans.
